❄️ What to wear Feb 15–21Peak winter — -5°C to 5°C with wind chill. Thermals are NON-NEGOTIABLE. Layer: thermal → fleece → heavy puffer. Waterproof snow boots (streets ice over at night). Gloves + beanie + neck gaiter. Hand warmers (핫팩) at every convenience store for ₩500–1,000.
🧳 Packing essentials
  • Thermal top + bottom (bring 2–3 sets each)
  • Heavy down or puffer jacket — non-negotiable
  • Waterproof gloves + beanie + neck gaiter
  • Waterproof snow boots — NOT regular sneakers
  • Lip balm + thick moisturizer — air is brutally dry
  • T-money card — subway, bus, some cafes (buy at airport)
  • Pocket hand warmers — life-saving at ski day and outdoor spots
