What to wear

Peak winter — -5°C to 5°C with wind chill. Thermals are non-negotiable. Layer: thermal top+bottom → fleece → heavy puffer jacket. Waterproof snow boots (not sneakers — streets ice over at night). Gloves, beanie, neck gaiter. Hand warmers (핫팩) are sold at every convenience store for ₩500–₩1,000.

Packing essentials ❄️

  • Thermal top + bottom (bring 2–3 sets each)
  • Heavy down or puffer jacket
  • Waterproof gloves + beanie + neck gaiter
  • Waterproof snow boots — NOT regular sneakers
  • Lip balm + thick moisturizer — air is brutally dry
  • T-money card — subway, bus, some cafes (buy at airport)
  • Pocket hand warmers — life-saving at ski day and outdoor activities
